This agent runs every week and finds people who recently changed jobs into roles you care about. It searches for matching profiles, enriches them with contact details, and emails you a structured report.

## What it does

1. Searches for people who started a new role in the past 3 months matching your criteria (title, industry, location, company size)
2. Enriches each contact with email, LinkedIn URL, and phone where available
3. Emails you a summary with a full results table — name, title, company, start date, and contact info

You define the criteria (job title, company size, location, industry) by editing the agent's instructions.

## Connectors

| Connector | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| People & Company Finder | Search for people who recently changed roles |
| Apollo | Enrich contacts with missing details |
| Exa | Web search fallback for finding LinkedIn profiles |
| Gmail | Send the weekly email report |

## How to run it

Runs automatically every Monday at 9am.
