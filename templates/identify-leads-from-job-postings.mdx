title: "Identify Leads from Job Postings"
description: "Use hiring activity as a buying signal to find warm leads"
---

If a company is hiring for a role your product serves, they're a warm lead. This agent scans job postings weekly, identifies companies actively hiring for relevant roles, finds the right person to reach out to at each one, and emails you the list.

## What it does

1. Searches for job postings from the past week matching a profession you define
2. Finds the right contact at each company (using titles like VP of Sales, CTO, Head of Marketing, etc.)
3. Emails you a lead table with: company, job they're hiring for, contact name, title, and LinkedIn URL

You define the profession and contact titles to target by editing the agent's instructions.

## Connectors

| Connector | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| Job Posting Search | Find companies actively hiring for your target roles |
| Web Scraper | Cover job postings not listed on major boards |
| Apollo | Find the right contact at each company |
| Exa | Web search fallback |
| Gmail | Send the weekly lead report |

## How to run it

Runs automatically every Monday at 9am.
