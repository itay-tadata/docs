Don't let the energy from a great meeting fade. This agent reviews your Granola meeting notes from the day, identifies who to follow up with, and drafts a warm, personalized LinkedIn message for each — ready in your Kondo drafts before the day ends.

## What it does

1. Fetches all of today's meetings from Granola
2. Identifies external attendees worth following up with
3. Checks whether you already have a LinkedIn thread with each person via Kondo
4. Drafts a short, personalized message referencing something specific from the meeting
5. Sends a Slack notification when drafts are ready, including messages for contacts without an existing thread

## Connectors

| Connector | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| Granola | Retrieve today's meeting notes and attendees |
| Kondo | Check existing threads and save message drafts |
| Slack | Notify you when drafts are ready to review |

## How to run it

Runs automatically every weekday at 6pm.
