This agent monitors job boards and LinkedIn every week for roles matching your criteria, and delivers a curated list straight to your inbox — with salary ranges, key skills, and direct application links.

## What it does

1. Searches career sites and LinkedIn for job postings from the past week matching your profession, location, and company size preferences
2. Deduplicates results across sources and filters out anything older than the search window
3. Emails you a summary with: job title, company, location, salary range (where listed), key skills, and a direct link to apply

You define the criteria (profession, location, company size, industry) by editing the agent's instructions.

## Connectors

| Connector | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| Job Posting Search | Search career sites and LinkedIn for job postings |
| Web Scraper | Find additional postings not listed on major job boards |
| Apollo | Find hiring managers at companies you want to apply to |
| Exa | Web search fallback |
| Gmail | Send the weekly email report |

## How to run it

Runs automatically every Monday at 9am.
