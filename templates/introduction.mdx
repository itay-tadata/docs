Templates are pre-built agents you can activate in minutes. Each one comes with a goal, instructions, and the right connectors already selected — you just authenticate and go.

<Card title="Browse Templates" icon="grid-2" href="https://app.tadata.com/templates">
  Open the template gallery in the platform
</Card>

## How to use a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a template">
    Browse the gallery and click on one that fits your use case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your accounts">
    Each template lists the services it needs. Click to authenticate — most use one-click OAuth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the instructions">
    The agent comes with a pre-written prompt. Edit it if you want to customize the behavior (optional).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it">
    Most templates are set up to run on a schedule automatically. You can also start a conversation with your agent at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
