Every new LinkedIn connection is a potential lead. This agent reviews your new connections daily, qualifies them against your ideal customer profile, researches their background, and drafts a personalized message — ready for you to review and send.

## What it does

1. Pulls all new LinkedIn connections from the past 24 hours
2. Researches each profile and qualifies them against your ICP criteria
3. Tags each connection with their ICP fit (strong, partial, or no fit) and a brief explanation
4. Drafts a personalized opening message for connections with an existing thread
5. Sends a Slack summary with qualified connections and proposed messages for new threads

## Setup

Before running, define your ICP criteria in the agent's instructions — the agent will prompt you if this hasn't been filled in yet.

## Connectors

| Connector | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| Kondo | Access LinkedIn connections, notes, and draft messages |
| LinkedIn Profile Scraper | Fetch full profile details for research |
| Web Search | Additional research to qualify each connection |
| Slack | Send a summary of qualified connections |

## How to run it

Runs automatically every day at 9am.
