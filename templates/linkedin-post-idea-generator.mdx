Every week, this agent reads your recent customer calls and internal meetings, scans the web for ecosystem signals, and synthesizes them into 3–6 LinkedIn post ideas your team is uniquely positioned to write. Ideas land in a Slack channel of your choice.

## What it does

1. Reads customer and prospect calls from the past 7 days, looking for recurring frustrations, workarounds, and aha moments
2. Reads internal meeting notes for the team's own observations about the market
3. Scans the web for early signals in your space
4. Synthesizes across all three sources to find non-obvious angles
5. Sends a Slack message with 3–6 post ideas — each with a working title, the core observation, relevant sources, and why your team is positioned to write it

You customize the company description, target audience, and any specific sources to monitor by editing the agent's instructions.

## Connectors

| Connector | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| Granola | Access customer call and internal meeting notes |
| Web Search | Scan for ecosystem signals and emerging trends |
| Slack | Deliver the weekly post ideas |

## How to run it

Runs automatically every Monday at 8am.
