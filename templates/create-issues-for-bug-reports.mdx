title: "Create Issues from Meeting Recordings"
description: "Automatically turn feature requests and bug reports from meetings into Linear issues"
---

This agent checks your meeting recordings every night, extracts any feature requests or bug reports mentioned, and creates well-structured Linear issues — so nothing slips through the cracks after a customer call or demo.

## What it does

1. Reviews meeting recordings and transcripts from the past 24 hours
2. Identifies feature requests, bug reports, and product improvement suggestions
3. For each one, creates a Linear issue with: a clear title, detailed description, who requested it, which meeting it came from, suggested priority, and appropriate labels
4. Checks for existing similar issues before creating new ones to avoid duplicates

## Connectors

| Connector | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| Fathom | Access meeting recordings and transcripts |
| Linear | Create and manage project issues |

## How to run it

Runs automatically every night at 2am.
