Clear your LinkedIn DM inbox faster. The agent reviews all your open message threads in Kondo and suggests the right action for each one — so you can work through your inbox systematically and reach Inbox 0.

## What it does

1. Pulls your full LinkedIn message inbox via Kondo
2. Reviews each thread and suggests an action (reply, archive, follow up, etc.)
3. Helps you work through your inbox to reach Inbox 0

## Connectors

| Connector | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| Kondo | Access and manage your LinkedIn message inbox |

## How to run it

Run on demand whenever you want to clear your inbox.
