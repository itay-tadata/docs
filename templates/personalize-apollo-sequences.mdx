Give this agent a list of prospects and your Apollo sequence ID, and it will research each person — their LinkedIn activity, recent posts, company news, and personal signals — then draft personalized messages for every customizable field. Nothing goes to Apollo until you approve it.

## What it does

1. Loads your Apollo sequence and identifies all customizable fields
2. Finds each prospect in Apollo (or creates them if needed)
3. Researches each person using LinkedIn, web search, and company signals to find specific, genuine details
4. Drafts personalized values for every field — short, grounded in real signals, never generic
5. Shows you a preview for each prospect and waits for your approval before uploading anything to Apollo

## Connectors

| Connector | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| Apollo | Load sequences, find/create contacts, upload personalized messages |
| LinkedIn Profile Scraper | Pull recent posts, activity, and career signals |
| Web Search | Find blog posts, podcast appearances, company news |

## How to run it

This agent is designed to run on demand. Start a conversation, provide your sequence ID and prospect list, and it walks you through the rest.
