Give this agent a conference URL and it will research who's attending — speakers and attendees — and compile them into a Google Sheet with names, titles, companies, and source links.

## What it does

1. Researches the conference website for speakers and attendees
2. Focuses only on the current year's event (ignores past editions)
3. Outputs a Google Sheet with columns: Name, Title, Company, Source URL

## Connectors

| Connector | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| Exa | Search and research conference attendee information |
| Google Sheets | Store the extracted attendee list |

## How to run it

This agent runs automatically every Monday at 9am. You can also start a conversation and give it a specific conference URL to run on demand.
