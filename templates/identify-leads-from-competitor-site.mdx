This agent visits a competitor's website, finds every customer story and case study, and extracts the people mentioned into a structured Google Sheet — with their name, company, title, and any quotes.

## What it does

1. Crawls the competitor's case study and customer story pages (follows pagination and "Load more" buttons)
2. Extracts every person mentioned: name, company, title, case study URL, and direct quotes
3. Outputs a complete Google Sheet and shares the link with you

## Connectors

| Connector | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| Web Search | Find and crawl the competitor's case study pages |
| Google Sheets | Store the extracted leads |

## How to run it

This agent is designed to be run on demand — start a conversation, give it the competitor's URL, and it will work through the site and deliver the sheet.
