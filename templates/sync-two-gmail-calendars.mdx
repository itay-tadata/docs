title: "Sync Two Gmail Calendars"
description: "Keep two Google Calendar accounts in sync automatically"
---

This agent keeps two Google Calendar accounts in sync — useful if you manage separate work and personal calendars and want one to reflect the other. It copies events across, avoids duplicates, and runs every few hours without any manual effort.

## What it does

On first run, it asks you a few quick questions:
- Which account to sync from (and to)
- Whether to sync one-way or two-way
- Whether to copy full event details or show events as "Busy" (for privacy)
- How far ahead to sync (up to 90 days)

After that, it saves your preferences and syncs automatically — no more questions unless you want to change a setting.

## Connectors

| Connector | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| Google Calendar (Account 1) | Read and write events from the first account |
| Google Calendar (Account 2) | Read and write events from the second account |

## How to run it

Runs automatically every 6 hours once configured.
