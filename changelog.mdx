<Update label="May 31 2026">
  ### New Features

  - **File Uploads in Chat** — You can now attach files directly in the chat input and share them with your agent.
  - **In-App Notifications** — A new bell icon in the header opens a notifications center. Get alerted when your agents complete runs, hit issues, or need your attention — and mark all as read in one click.
  - **Mobile Support** — Tadata now works on mobile browsers, so you can chat with your agents wherever you are.

  ### Improvements

  - **Live Status Indicators** — Agents and conversations in the sidebar now show a status dot so you can see at a glance what's running or idle.
  - **Hover to Preview Run Outcomes** — Hover over any agent or conversation in the sidebar to peek at recent results without opening it.
  - **Resizable Sidebar** — The threads sidebar can now be resized to fit your preferred layout.
  - **Bearer Auth Auto-Attach** — When configuring a private connector that uses header-based authentication, the `Bearer` prefix is now added automatically.
</Update>

<Update label="May 12 2026">
  ### New Features

  - **Out-of-Credits Notifications** — If your agent has a scheduled trigger but can't run due to insufficient credits, you'll receive a one-time email notification.
</Update>

<Update label="May 7 2026">
  ### New Features

  - **Pause & Resume Triggers** — You can now pause and resume your agent triggers without deleting them. Put a trigger on hold and pick it back up whenever you're ready.
</Update>

<Update label="May 6 2026">
  ### New Features

  - **Voice Dictation** — You can now talk to Tadata! Just click the microphone button in the chat input and say what you need — Tadata will be happy to help.

  ### New Connectors

  - **Otter** — Capture and search meeting notes, transcripts, and action items from your Otter conversations.
  - **Circleback** — Access AI-generated meeting notes, summaries, and action items from your Circleback meetings.
  - **Amplitude** — Query your product analytics data, including events, user behavior, and funnel metrics.
  - **Todoist** — Manage tasks and projects in Todoist — create, update, and organize your to-dos.
</Update>

<Update label="May 2 2026">
  ### New Connectors

  - **LinkedIn Posts Scraper** — Scrape LinkedIn posts from member and company feeds, LinkedIn search, or specific post URLs in real time.
  - **X Posts Scraper** — Search and scrape tweets from X (Twitter) using advanced search-style queries, including threads and high-volume exports.
</Update>

<Update label="April 13 2026">
  ### New Connectors

  - **Kyckr** — Search and verify companies across 120+ countries for KYB/KYC compliance.
  - **Kondo** — Manage all your DMs and chat inboxes in one place, for you and your AI agents.

  ### New Templates

  - **Warm Intro Finder** — Find the best LinkedIn intro paths from your team to any target contact, including second-degree connections through colleagues at the target's company.
  - **Turn New LinkedIn Connections Into Prospects** — Automatically qualify new LinkedIn connections against your ICP and draft personalized outreach messages, daily.
  - **Kondo Inbox 0** — Review your LinkedIn message inbox and clear it faster with suggested actions for each thread.
  - **Follow-up on Today's Calls** — Review your Granola meetings and draft warm LinkedIn follow-up messages in Kondo, ready before the day ends.
</Update>

<Update label="March 9 2026">
  ### New Features

  - **Claude Code** - Tadata agents now run on Claude Code under the hood. Agents are capable of bigger, more complex tasks — they'll stay on track with a to-do list, use per-conversation memory, and write scripts to bridge any gaps needed to accomplish their goals.
  - **Slack-style agent navigation** - Agents now live in the left sidebar as persistent collaborators, much like a Slack DM thread with a teammate you work with regularly.
  - **Agent identity in chat** - The chat sidebar now shows the agent's instructions and connectors while it runs, so you always have context on what your agent is working with.
  - **Delete connectors** - You can now remove connectors from an agent, making it easier to iterate on your setup without starting from scratch.

  ### Improvements

  - Better handling of large data responses from connectors such as Gmail and Fathom.
</Update>

<Update label="February 22 2026">
  ### New Features

  - **Templates** - Grab a pre-built template inside Tadata for common workflows: meeting prep, documenting feature requests from video calls, and identifying conference leads. We've also posted build-along demos to our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@TadataHQ)
  - **Webhooks** - Tadata agents can now be triggered via external HTTP webhooks, allowing third-party services and applications to programmatically invoke agents with custom payloads.
  - **Duplicate agents** – Clone any agent, including its auth configuration.

  ### Connector Updates

  **Connector Improvements**
  - HubSpot: Now supports write operations

  **Newly Added Connectors**

  - *Notetakers* - Granola
  - *Databases* - Weaviate
  - *Sales & Lead Intelligence* - LinkedIn Post Comments, LinkedIn Post Reactions
</Update>

<Update label="February 12 2026">
  ### New Features

  - **Prompt to Create an Agent** - Describe what you want to accomplish and Tadata will automatically expand your prompt and suggest the right connectors to get the job done.
  - **Generate Missing Connectors** - Don't see a connector you need? Go to `Connectors` > `+ Add new connector` > `Generate with AI`. Then enter the connector name and Tadata will generate a connector for it in ~5 minutes. Keep the tab open while it works.

  ### Connector Updates

  **Connector Improvements**
  - Gmail: Added draft tool + improved handling of longer messages
  - Fathom: Better support for longer transcripts

  **Newly Added Connectors**

  - *Sales & Lead Intelligence* - Apollo.io, HeyReach, Hunter.io, Lemlist, LinkedIn, RocketReach, UpLead, Voila Norbert
  - *Forms & Surveys*  - Jotform, Typeform
  - *Communication* - Outlook Mail
  - *Security & Compliance* - CIRCL Vulnerability DB, CISA KEV, OpenSSF Scorecard
  - *Platform Integrations* - Mixpanel, Webflow

  ### Bug Fixes

  - Agent-creation progress now saves when you resize your browser window. No losing progress with the message that Tadata is intended for desktop screens.
</Update>
